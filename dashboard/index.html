The value model

Huber log-log regression on traded comparables: ln(market cap) on ln(TVL), ln(chains) and 7-day growth. A 6-specification horse race (Method tab) found nothing that beats it on leave-one-out and walk-forward at once.

The odds model

Logistic regression on peers. . The fitted probability survives as the quality ratio: a protocol's own fitted odds over its age bucket's rate.

The conformal 50% band is ± in log space; measured coverage on the comparables is (the band is honest about how wide it is — rank ranges on every row come from 200 bootstrap refits).

Conditional odds — P(ever launches | tokenless at this age)

A protocol that is still tokenless at age t has already survived the launches its cohort already had. The cumulative share counts those launches; the conditional odds remove them: (F∞ − F(t)) / (1 − F(t)), with F∞ = 0.95. F(t) is the share of protocols already tokened by each age, measured on 525 peers. The 4–5y and 5y+ buckets are pooled (n = 49) because the raw 5y+ bucket held only 6 protocols and broke the monotone fall. Protocols with no listing date use the cohort base rate (59%).

F∞ = 0.95 is the midpoint of the honest range [0.90, 1.00]: the current-status likelihood rises monotonically to the 1.00 boundary, so the data barely pins F∞ down — the full profile is in the Method tab, and the top 3 are identical at every F∞ in [0.90, 1.00].

Method

The value model

A rival ships only when it beats the shipped spec on leave-one-out and walk-forward. None does: OLS wins walk-forward and loses leave-one-out; adding age wins neither. The flat baseline — one global median mcap/TVL multiple for every protocol — misses by ×4.60.

The odds model

The cure fraction

Every protocol either launches eventually (fraction F∞) or never (1 − F∞). The current-status likelihood — each peer contributes F(t) if tokened, 1 − F(t) if not, with F(t) = F∞·G(t) and G a logistic curve in ln(years) — rises monotonically to the 1.00 boundary, so F∞ is not identified. The point estimate 0.95 is a choice, the midpoint of [0.90, 1.00], and the board barely moves across the range:

The fit, seen

Each dot is one of the 94 comparables (ln TVL vs ln market cap); the line is the Huber fit at the median chain count and median 7-day growth, the band is the conformal ±. R² is only 0.11 — the dashboard says so on the cover and carries the uncertainty as an interval and a bootstrap rank range on every row.

Honest limits

  • The value fit is weak (R² ~ 0.11); rank ranges are wide. Treat ranks as a shortlist, not a prophecy.
  • F∞ is a choice, not a measurement (see above).
  • Age is a proxy for launch timing; funding rounds and team decisions are invisible in the snapshot.
  • Survivorship: dead protocols leave the snapshot; F(t) is measured on survivors only.
  • Farm economics are a plan, not a promise — the sensitivity grid on each detail view shows what happens when the 7% / 0.02% / $960 assumptions are wrong.
  • Own-token share is a snapshot proxy for family structure (first slug segment), not a legal fact.
